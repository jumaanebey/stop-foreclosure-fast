The timeline, locally

How the process runs in Alameda County

Recording starts the clock

An East Bay foreclosure begins when the Notice of Default is recorded with the county — the Alameda County Clerk-Recorder in Oakland, or the Contra Costa County Clerk-Recorder in Martinez. From that date you have at least 90 days with a statutory right to reinstate: bring the loan current and the foreclosure ends.

Notice of Trustee's Sale — 21 days minimum

After the reinstatement period, a Notice of Trustee's Sale can set an auction no sooner than 21 days out. The date, time, and place are on the notice. Postponements are granted regularly when there's a complete modification application, an active escrow, or an attorney engaged — but someone has to ask.

If there are tenants in the property

Oakland is a city of duplexes and in-law units, and foreclosure doesn't erase tenant rights. California law and Oakland's Just Cause for Eviction ordinance give tenants real protections after a foreclosure sale, including notice requirements. If you're a landlord facing foreclosure with tenants in place — or a tenant in a foreclosed building — that's a situation where I'll point you to an attorney early. We go through your notices and your numbers together and map every option: reinstatement, a repayment plan, a loan modification, or — only if it's genuinely right — a sale that converts Bay Area equity into your next chapter instead of losing it at auction. Resources you should use

HUD-approved housing counselors — free

Call (800) 569-4287 for a HUD-approved counseling agency serving Alameda or Contra Costa County. Free, legitimate, and a good check on anything anyone — including me — tells you.

County court self-help and legal aid

The Alameda County Superior Court's self-help center can explain court procedures at no cost, and East Bay legal aid organizations offer free housing-law help to eligible residents of both counties.
