The timeline, locally

How foreclosure runs in Sacramento County

Ninety days that belong to you

When the Notice of Default is recorded with the Sacramento County Clerk/Recorder, California law hands you at least 90 days in which you can reinstate — pay the arrears and stop the foreclosure cold. That window is a right, not a favor, and it's the best time to get a modification application in front of your servicer.

Then 21 days' minimum notice of sale

A Notice of Trustee's Sale follows if nothing changes, setting an auction at least 21 days out at the location printed on the notice. The same rules apply whether your home is in the city, in Elk Grove, or across the river in West Sacramento — though a different county recorder (Yolo, Placer, or El Dorado) may hold the paperwork.

The clock rewards early movers

Every option — reinstatement, repayment plan, modification, or a sale on your terms — gets easier the further you are from the auction date. See the whole sequence, stage by stage, on the California foreclosure timeline.

The capital has resources — use them

HUD-approved housing counselors — free

Call (800) 569-4287. As the state capital, the Sacramento region is well served by HUD-approved counseling agencies whose foreclosure-prevention advice is free. There is no version of your situation where a free second opinion hurts you.

Court self-help and legal services

The Sacramento Superior Court's self-help center explains court procedures at no charge, and area legal services organizations provide free housing-law help to eligible residents of Sacramento, Yolo, Placer, and El Dorado counties.
