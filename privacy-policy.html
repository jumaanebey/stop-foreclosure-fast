Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information My Foreclosure Solution ("we," "us") collects through myforeclosuresolution.com, how it's used, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect only what you give us and basic analytics, we use it to respond to you, and we do not sell it.

What we collect

  • Information you submit — name, phone number, email address, property city, and details about your foreclosure situation when you fill out a form, call, or text.
  • Usage information — standard analytics data (pages visited, device type, approximate location from IP) collected via cookies by Google Analytics and, where enabled, the Meta pixel.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide the consultation you requested.
  • To prepare and follow through on a plan you've asked for, including — with your consent — sharing your information with licensed professionals or attorneys working on your file.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not place your contact information into shared lead pools or distribute it to third-party marketers.
  • We do not share your situation with anyone not directly involved in helping you, except as required by law.

Service providers

The site relies on a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf: website hosting, form delivery to our inbox, and analytics (Google Analytics; Meta pixel where enabled). Each receives only what it needs to perform its function.

Cookies and analytics

Analytics cookies help us see how the site is used. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site works without them. Google provides an opt-out browser add-on for Google Analytics.

Data retention

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to help you and to meet legal and record-keeping obligations, then delete it.

Your California privacy rights

California residents may request to know what personal information we hold about them, request a copy of it, request deletion, and direct us not to share it. To exercise any of these rights, email help@myforeclosuresolution.com — we'll respond within the timeframes California law requires, and we will never treat you differently for asking.

Children

This site is for adults dealing with home foreclosure and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
